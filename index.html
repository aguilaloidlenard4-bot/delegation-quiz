The Five Rights of Delegation

Check your understanding of safe delegation in nursing practice: who may do a task, under what conditions, and how the nurse stays accountable for the outcome.

Learning objectives

  • Define delegation and explain where accountability rests.
  • Identify tasks that can be safely delegated to assistive personnel.
  • Apply each of the Five Rights to clinical situations.
  • Recognize unsafe delegation practices.

The Five Rights

  1. Right task
  2. Right circumstance
  3. Right person
  4. Right direction and communication
  5. Right supervision and evaluation
